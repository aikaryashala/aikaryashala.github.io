Assessment & Placement

Assessing skills. Connecting talent with opportunity.

new initiative

A resume or a certificate only says so much. This initiative evaluates what a candidate can actually do — through structured assessments that cover fundamentals, applied AI workflows, and problem-solving under real constraints, not just multiple-choice recall.

Once skills are assessed, we work on the other half of the problem: connecting verified talent with the companies and teams that need it. The goal is a shorter, more honest path between being ready and being hired.

Who it's for

Candidate who wants their practical skills assessed and connected to the right opportunities.

Companies and teams looking to hire talent with verified skills, without the noise of resumes and interviews that don't reflect actual ability.

Colleges and universities that want to provide their students with a more practical, skills-based path to employment, beyond traditional academic credentials.

Get in touch

Want to be assessed, or looking to hire assessed talent? Reach out and we'll take it from there.
